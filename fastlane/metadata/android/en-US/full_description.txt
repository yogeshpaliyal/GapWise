When did you last get a haircut? Water the plants? Call home? GapWise remembers, so you don't have to.

Tap once when you do something. GapWise counts the days since, learns how often you usually do it, and gives you a gentle nudge around when it's due again. There are no streaks to break and nothing to feel guilty about.

<b>Remember the last time</b>
• One tap to log, with undo
• Log a past day, add a note, edit any entry
• Events that last days, like a trip or a sick day, get a start and an end

<b>Know the next</b>
• After a few logs, GapWise learns your rhythm, like "usually every 36 days"
• One-off long gaps, like a holiday, don't throw it off
• Each event's icon changes shape as time passes, so you can see what's due at a glance

<b>From your home screen</b>
• Pick 1 to 12 events for a tile widget and log any of them with one tap
• A "due soon" list widget
• Launcher shortcuts for your most used events

<b>Private by design</b>
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Back up to a private folder in your own Google Drive, or export to a file
• Open source: github.com/yogeshpaliyal/Gapwise
